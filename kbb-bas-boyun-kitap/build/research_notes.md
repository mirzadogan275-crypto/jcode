## ATA 2025 DTC guideline (Ringel MD et al., Thyroid 2025; doi:10.1177/10507256251363120) — nodules EXCLUDED (separate guideline)
- 84 recs (2015: 101). Patient advocate in panel. GRADE: strong/conditional/GPS.
- Rec1: WHO 5th ed low-risk neoplasms (NIFTP, FT-UMP, hyalinizing trabecular tumor) — no completion/LND/RAI routinely.
- Germline testing if syndromic suspicion: PTEN (Cowden), DICER1 (pediatric familial), APC (cribriform-morular TC), Carney, Werner. FNMTC US screening if ≥3 affected relatives.
- Rec6: high-volume surgeon (>25–50 thyroidectomies/yr) – strong.
- Rec7: preop US central+lateral nodes + gross ETE; FNA suspicious nodes >8–10 mm smallest diameter; Tg washout.
- Rec8: contrast CT/MRI neck+mediastinum if advanced/invasive; chest/abd/pelvis imaging if changes plan (GPS); routine preop FDG-PET not advised.
- Rec9 no routine preop Tg/TgAb. Rec10 no routine preop genomic testing.
- Rec11: active surveillance option for cT1aN0M0 PTC (conditional); US-guided percutaneous ablation alternative (conditional). Rec12 US follow-up; Rec13 no Tg; Rec14 surgery if: biopsy+ LN, growth >3 mm, distant mets, ETE, posterior growth, patient concern/pref, non-adherence.
- Rec15A: <2 cm cT1N0M0 no gross ETE → LOBECTOMY first choice (strong). 15B: 2–4 cm cT2N0M0 low-risk unilateral → lobectomy MAY be preferred (conditional); TT if contralateral nodules/preference. 15C: >4 cm (cT3a), gross ETE (cT3b/T4), cN1 or cM1 → TT + lymph node dissection.
- Rec16 completion thyroidectomy conditional.
- Rec17-18 thyroglossal duct carcinoma: Sistrunk; + TT if suspicious thyroid nodularity / advanced; completion if Delphian LN mets / high-risk.
- Rec19: prophylactic CND NOT recommended for FTC or cT1–T2 cN0 PTC (strong); may be considered T3–T4 cN0. Central compartment = level VI + upper VII.
- Rec20: cN1a → therapeutic CND; cN1b → ipsilateral central + lateral ND (IIa, III, IV, Vb) + TT.
- Rec22: voice eval all; laryngoscopy if dysphonia, prior neck/upper thoracic surgery, extensive central / posterior ETE / lateral mets.
- Rec23: visual RLN ID; IONM may be used (esp. total/reoperative); EBSLN monitoring; check RLN signal after first side before contralateral (staged if LOS).
- Rec24: parathyroid autotransplant after frozen confirmation; PTH-directed Ca/vitD.
- Rec25: routine drains not recommended. Rec26: post-op voice eval, SLP referral for RLN injury.
- New ATA risk stratification system (RSS) w/ AJCC8: separate tracks for papillary, follicular, oncocytic; 4 risk categories. (VERIFY names)

## ATA 2025 – postoperative (Uludağ et al. SEMB 2025 Part 2; doi:10.14744/SEMB.2025.30906)
- New 2025 ATA RSS: separate tracks PTC / FTC(+IEFVPTC) / OTC; 4 categories: LOW, LOW-INTERMEDIATE, INTERMEDIATE-HIGH, HIGH. Uses histology, LN number, AJCC, postop imaging, Tg/TgAb (first ~3 months).
- Response to therapy (excellent, indeterminate, biochemical incomplete, structural incomplete) applied already within first 3 mo (dynamic RS earlier).
- Tg 6–12 weeks after TT; after lobectomy single Tg at 6-12 wk (no cutoff).
- US 6–12 mo after initial therapy; suspicious LN <8–10 mm can be observed; ≥8–10 mm FNA + Tg washout.
- FDG-PET for OTC/PDTC with rising Tg & negative US.
- RAI: low-risk → NO routine remnant ablation (strong, high certainty); low-intermediate & intermediate-high → may consider adjuvant RAI (conditional); high → routine adjuvant RAI; distant mets → RAI routinely. OTC: no definitive rec; diagnostic WBS to check avidity.
- Preparation: rhTSH PREFERRED over withdrawal (strong, high); LT4 withdrawal ≥3–4 wk (LT3 bridge stop ≥2 wk before); TSH >30 mIU/L; low-iodine diet 1–2 wk; post-therapy WBS ± SPECT/CT.
- Pregnancy avoid ≥6 months after RAI; breastfeeding stop ≥3 mo before; men cumulative >400 mCi (14.8 GBq) infertility counselling.
- EBRT adjuvant may be considered for high-risk locoregional features not amenable to salvage (gross ETE, aggressive subtype, + margins).
- WHO 2022 terms: oncocytic (not Hürthle), high-grade follicular cell-derived non-anaplastic (PDTC + DHGTC), IEFVPTC, widely invasive FTC; aggressive PTC subtypes: tall cell, columnar, hobnail.

## AJCC/UICC TNM-9 HPV-associated (p16+) oropharynx (Version 9 effective Jan 1, 2026; UICC 9th published July 2025)
- cTNM9: cT unchanged; clinical stage grouping unchanged; iENE = new cN modifier → upstage ONE N category: ipsilateral ≤6 cm + iENE → cN2; bilateral/contralateral ≤6 cm + iENE → cN3. (8th: cN1 ipsi ≤6cm; cN2 contra/bilat ≤6; cN3 >6cm)
- iENE definition: unequivocal radiologic extension beyond capsule into perinodal fat/adjacent structures (skin, muscle, NV) OR coalescent nodal mass (≥2 nodes, lost planes).
- Clinical stages (unchanged): I T0-2 N0-1; II T0-2 N2 or T3 N0-2; III T4 any N or any T N3; IV M1.
- pTNM9 (Lancet Oncol 2025; doi:10.1016/S1470-2045(25)00281-5): pN1a = 1 LN, ENE−; pN1b = 2–4 LN, ENE−; pN2 = >4 LN ENE− OR 1–4 LN ENE+; pN3 = >4 LN ENE+.
  Path stages: I T0-2 N0-1; II T0-2 N2-3 or T3 N0-2; III T3N3 or T4 N0-3; IV M1. Distribution I 55% II 41% III 4%. pENE HR 1.47; minor vs major not different.
- (AJCC8 path: pN1 ≤4 LN; pN2 >4 LN; stage I T0-2N0-1; II T0-2N2, T3-4N0-1; III T3-4N2)

## AJCC/UICC TNM-9 Salivary gland (Version 9 Jan 1 2026; doi:10.1245/s10434-026-19350-5)
- Major + minor SGC unified; excludes SCC, neuroendocrine ca, basosquamous; carcinoma ex PA no longer separate type (classified by carcinoma type/grade); low vs high-risk tiers by grade/subtype.
- T unchanged: T1 ≤2 cm; T2 >2–4; T3 >4 cm or GROSS extraparenchymal extension (micro alone insufficient); T4a skin, mandible (bone invasion), ear canal, facial nerve (named nerve); T4b skull base, pterygoid plates, encases carotid. Also covers trachea, cervical esophagus, middle ear, intraosseous jaw SGC.
- N: N0; N1 = 1–3 LN without ENE; N2 = >3 LN OR any ENE (counts parotid + cervical nodes); cN = pN definitions.
- Stages: I T1N0; II T2N0; IIIA T1-2N1 or T3-4N0; IIIB any N2 or T3-4N1; IV M1.
- (UICC9 overview: Turk Arch Otorhinolaryngol 2025 doi:10.4274/tao.2025.2025-11-14)

## Trials (verified)
- KEYNOTE-689 (NEJM July 2025; Uppaluri): resectable stage III–IVA LA-HNSCC (oral cavity, larynx, hypopharynx, p16− OPC); neoadj pembro 200 mg ×2 → surgery → adjuvant RT/CRT + pembro (total 15 adj cycles). EFS CPS≥10 HR 0.66; CPS≥1 median EFS 59.7 vs 29.6 mo. FDA approval June 12, 2025 (CPS≥1). First positive in >20 yr.
- NIVOPOSTOP (GORTEC 2018-01; ASCO 2025; Lancet 2026): high-risk resected (ENE, R1, ≥ multiple nodes...) cisplatin-RT ± nivolumab; 3-yr DFS 63.1% vs 52.5%; HR 0.76 (0.60–0.98) p=.034; benefit irrespective of PD-L1.
- NRG-HN005 (ASTRO 2024): p16+ OPC non-smoker T1-2N1/T3N0-1 (AJCC8); 70 Gy+cis vs 60 Gy+cis vs 60 Gy+nivo; 2-yr PFS 98.1% vs 88.6% vs 90.3% → neither de-escalation non-inferior → 70 Gy+cisplatin remains SOC.
- PATHOS (UK): TORS/TLM → low risk none; intermediate 60 vs 50 Gy; high risk CRT vs RT alone (ENE+/margin). Final results pending as of Oct 2026 (not found).
- C-POST (NEJM 2025): adjuvant cemiplimab after surgery+RT high-risk cSCC; DFS HR 0.32; FDA approval Oct 8, 2025 (350 mg q3w ×4 → 700 mg q6w ×6).
- JCOG1008: weekly cisplatin 40 mg/m2 noninferior to 100 mg/m2 q3w postoperative high-risk.

## CAP 2025 HPV testing guideline update
- OPSCC tissue (incl. LN mets w/ OP clinical): p16 IHC; positive = ≥70% nuclear + cytoplasmic, moderate–strong.
- HPV-specific testing (ISH/PCR) if: low HPV-prevalence region; equivocal p16 (50–70% or extensive weak); p16-morphology discordance; large multisite tumors overlapping OP; non-tonsil/non-BOT OP sites.
- FNA (cytology): reflex HPV-SPECIFIC testing rather than p16 (strong) — change from 2018.
- Sinonasal SCC: test directly for transcriptionally active HR-HPV (RNA-ISH); p16 can screen.
- NCCN 1-2.2026: p16 70% cutoff w/ nuclear+cytoplasmic moderate-strong.

## ISSVA 2025 (Paris, May 2025): tumors vs malformations vs PUVA (provisionally unclassified); malformations by FLOW (fast-flow, slow-flow, anomalies of named vessels); "simple" term removed; PIK3CA lesions grouped under slow-flow; "intramuscular fast-flow vascular anomaly" replaces intramuscular capillary-type hemangioma; glossary added.
